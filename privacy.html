This is a personal portfolio site. It collects no accounts, no sign-ups, and no tracking of its own. Here is exactly what happens with your data.

Collection

What this site collects

This portfolio has no accounts, no sign-ups, and no analytics of its own. The only personal information you may share is what you choose to send through the contact page:

  • Contact form: when you submit the form, your name, email address, and message are forwarded to the site owner through a third-party form service (formsubmit.io) so your message can be answered. Nothing is stored in a database on this site.
  • Email and phone links: tapping the email or phone links on the contact page opens your own mail app or dialer. No information is recorded by this site in that process.

Messages are used only to reply to you. They are never sold, rented, or shared with marketers.

Cookies

Cookies and advertising

This site shows ads through Google AdSense (publisher ca-pub-1184879199231987). Google uses cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Learn more in Google's advertising policy.

  • Opting out: you can turn off personalized ads at any time in your Google Ads Settings or in your browser's cookie controls.
  • Site preferences: your theme (light, dark, or anime) and anime sub-mode choices are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Rights

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of any message you sent, ask for a correction, or ask that your message be deleted. To do so, reach out through the contact page and the request will be honored.

This site is a general-audience portfolio and is not aimed at children. If you believe a child has shared personal information through the contact form, reach out and it will be removed.

Changes

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site changes, for example if new features or services are added. The "Last updated" date below will always show the most recent version.

These are standard terms prepared for a personal portfolio; for full legal protection you may want a lawyer to review them.
